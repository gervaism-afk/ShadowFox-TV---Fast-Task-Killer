Five new tools in Ultimate Center:

- **Network → Stability Test:** twelve TCP connection checks, showing typical response time, variation, failed attempts, and network changes. The test can be cancelled. It does not measure download speed or packet loss, and does not test your streaming provider.
- **Apps → Favorite:** pin preferred apps at the top of the existing app list. Favorites are saved between sessions.
- **System → Health & Support → Low-storage alerts:** optional notifications with a choice of storage threshold and a shortcut to system storage settings. Storage is checked periodically and when ShadowFox opens.
- **System → Health & Support → Heat warnings:** optional notifications based on Android's thermal status, while ShadowFox is open, on Android 10 and later. Firmware reporting varies.
- **System → Health & Support → Preview Support Report:** review device/app details and recent diagnostics, then copy, save, or share the report. Save writes to Downloads/ShadowFox on Android TV 10+; phones and tablets use the system file picker. Nothing is uploaded automatically. Sharing choices depend on the apps installed on your device.

Alerts start switched off. Enabling notifications may require Android permission. Warnings use recovery thresholds and cooldowns to avoid repeated alerts.

The approved main dashboard, artwork, optimizer behavior, and Google TV Streamer non-root policy are preserved. Other devices retain root detection.
