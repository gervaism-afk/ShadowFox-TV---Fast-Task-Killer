Apps guidance and reliability improvements:

- Open **Ultimate Center → Apps → Help** for explanations of Launch, Favorite, Protect, Stream, Settings, Uninstall, and Running/Idle indicators.
- The guide explains the difference between favorites and cleanup protection, plus Google TV Streamer's non-root limitations. It supports scrolling by touch and focus navigation with a TV remote.
- Protect/Unprotect refreshes the app's displayed protection state immediately.
- Launch and Stream support apps that expose only an Android TV launcher entry. Launch failures are reported instead of failing silently.
- Streaming results remain visible above the app list when you return to ShadowFox.

The approved main dashboard, artwork, and device root policies are preserved.
